Questions

Common questions

Do you guarantee search rankings or traffic?

No. Rankings and traffic depend on many factors outside our control. We deliver the agreed work and report on it plainly.

What happens after launch?

We hand over the site with notes so your team can run it. Growth includes 14 days of support. Ongoing care and further changes are scoped and priced separately.

What if I need something that is not in the package?

We tell you what it would involve and quote it in writing. Nothing outside the agreed scope is done without your approval.

What is the difference between Digital Start and Launch?

Digital Start puts your business online quickly on a proven, mobile-first layout filled with your content. Launch is designed from scratch around your brand and goes live on your own domain. Many businesses start with Digital Start and move up later.

How does payment work?

Digital Start is paid upfront. Launch and Growth are a deposit to start and the balance on delivery, set out in your written agreement. Custom platforms are paid by milestone. In India you can pay by UPI or bank transfer; from abroad, by international bank transfer. Nothing starts until you have agreed the scope and price.

When do I need Custom Platform instead of Growth?

If you need bookings, payments, accounts, dashboards or anything that stores and processes data, it is a custom platform. We start with a discovery call to define positioning and requirements, then quote a fixed price.

Who owns the website and code?

You do, at handover. That includes design files, code and content.

Which currency do you work in?

Prices are in Indian rupees. Clients abroad can pay the US dollar equivalent shown next to each price. Taxes and payment fees are stated in the agreement.
