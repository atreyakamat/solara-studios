About

A small studio in Goa with a short menu

Solara Studios exists to give businesses a website that works as a dependable part of how they operate.

The studio is led by Atreya Kamat, a software engineer who builds the sites, runs the technical SEO work and talks to clients directly. There is no handoff between a sales team and a delivery team.

We work with businesses that need a new or improved site, and with teams that need a custom booking, ordering or analytics platform. Examples of past projects are on the home page and the portfolio.

Principles

How the studio behaves

Outcomes over decoration

The work is tied to a real customer or operational need, not to how a page looks in isolation.

Clear scope

Deliverables, exclusions, revision limits and acceptance criteria are written down first.

Reliable delivery

We only commit to dates that match our capacity, and we say early when something is at risk.

Honest marketing

We do not invent testimonials, case studies or performance claims.

Long-term trust

We would rather hand over something you understand than create a dependency.

Careful with access

Credentials are shared securely, and access is limited to what the work needs.
