Since 1886 · Three rooms · Assagao

Bem-vindo, welcome home.A Portuguese-Goan house with blue tiles, a long balcão and nowhere to rush.

Casa Azulejo is our family home, restored room by room. Wake up to the church bell from St Cajetan's, eat poi and chorizo on the balcão, and be at Anjuna or Vagator in fifteen minutes.

A day at Casa Azulejo, at Goa's pace

7 am

The poder's horn comes down the lane. Fresh poi and kakon on the breakfast table, with tea or Goan coffee.

10 am

Scooter to Vagator, or a slow walk to St Cajetan's and the paddy fields behind it.

4 pm

Back for a nap under the fan, then bebinca and cutlets on the balcão while the light goes gold.

8 pm

Dinner in Assagao's cafés, five minutes away. Ask us which ones the locals actually go to.
