Privacy policy

Last updated 13 October 2026. Plain-language summary of how this website handles your information.

Who we are

Solara Studios is a small web studio in Goa, India, led by Atreya Kamat. Contact: atkamat1204@gmail.com.

What we collect

Only what you send us through the contact form or by email: your name, email address, company, website, package of interest and your message.

How we use it

To reply to your enquiry, prepare a scope and quote, and run a project if you go ahead. We do not sell your information and we do not send marketing emails you did not ask for.

Where it goes

Form submissions are received by our hosting provider, Netlify, and delivered to our email. Pages load fonts from Google Fonts, which means your browser contacts Google when you visit. This site does not currently set tracking cookies or run advertising trackers.

How long we keep it

We keep enquiries only as long as needed to respond and, for clients, to run and support the project.

Your choices

  • Ask what we hold about you.
  • Ask us to correct or delete it.
